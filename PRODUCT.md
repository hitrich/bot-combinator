# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Bot Combinator is for a solo founder or CEO running a US-focused pre-seed, seed, or Series A raise. They work under time pressure, move between research, introductions, direct outreach, meetings, diligence, and allocation, and need one trustworthy local system instead of a spreadsheet-and-inbox patchwork. The primary task on any screen is to decide and complete the safest, highest-leverage next action for the round.

## Product Purpose

Bot Combinator is a free, Apache-2.0, local-first Electron fundraising operating system. It joins an evidence-backed investor graph to a founder-controlled CRM, explains investor fit, preserves source history, coordinates warm paths and approved direct outreach, and keeps all private activity in a single local SQLite vault. Success means a founder can move from round definition to a prioritized, cited target list and a disciplined live process without a hosted account, mandatory paid service, or accidental spam.

## Brand Personality

Exact, calm, resourceful. Bot Combinator speaks like a prepared chief of staff: candid about uncertainty, terse when action is obvious, and rigorous when evidence or external communication is involved. It should create confidence and momentum without manufacturing urgency.

## Anti-references

- Generic CRM dashboards that turn every fact into a card and every workflow into configuration.
- AI-purple gradients, neon accents, glassmorphism, decorative agent theater, and chat-first interfaces that hide the underlying records.
- Finance-terminal cosplay, navy-and-gold prestige styling, or dark-mode-only density.
- Growth-hacking and bulk-email products that reward volume, vanity metrics, or unattended sequences.
- Consumer-playful interfaces that make consequential outreach feel casual.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence travels with the decision.** Fit, check size, partner focus, conflicts, and relationship claims always expose provenance and freshness.
2. **One clear next action.** Each workspace state makes the founder's next safe move obvious without erasing context.
3. **Dense, calm, and inspectable.** Use familiar product patterns, compact information, progressive disclosure, and stable spatial structure.
4. **External actions feel consequential.** Sending, requesting an introduction, sharing documents, overriding suppression, and exporting private data require explicit, legible review.
5. **Local trust is visible.** Storage, connector access, agent disclosure, backup, and privacy status are understandable from the interface rather than buried in policy text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA across the supported desktop surfaces. All workflows must be keyboard-complete and screen-reader legible; focus must never be hidden; status may not depend on color alone; body and placeholder text must meet contrast requirements; tables must preserve usable reading order; motion must respect reduced-motion settings; zoom to 200% must not lose functionality; and touch targets must remain practical on convertible Windows devices.
