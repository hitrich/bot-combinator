# Liquidity and launch readiness

Status: preview guidance

The CRM records plans, simulations, approvals, and read-only outcomes. Project-controlled wallets and Safes retain financial authority.

## Liquidity plan

- Verified assets/pair and environment.
- Project-reported budget and treasury authority.
- Scenario inputs, ranges, assumptions, and method version.
- Expected depth and price impact at agreed quote sizes.
- Concentration, volatility, and operational risks.

## Policy and approval

- Permitted operational bounds.
- Required project and Klineo reviewer roles.
- Pause, escalation, and rollback conditions.
- Separation between operational approval and wallet authorization.

## Launch runbook

- Owner and launch window.
- Integration, liquidity, security, monitoring, communication, and reporting gates.
- Exact source/freshness checks before launch.
- Incident contacts and stop conditions.
- Post-launch reconciliation and outcome review.

Never promise token price, volume, adoption, or financial return. Never create artificial or deceptive trading activity.
